## I cannot find Facebetter Beauty in Final Cut Pro

1. Open **Facebetter FCP Beauty.app** and finish **Set up for Final Cut**.
2. Fully quit Final Cut Pro (not only close the project window), then reopen it.
3. In the Effects browser, search for **Facebetter Beauty**.

Still stuck? Email [hello@facebetter.net](mailto:hello@facebetter.net).

## Does this work on Intel Macs?

No. The Final Cut / Motion plugin currently supports **Apple silicon** only.
