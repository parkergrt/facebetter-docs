Apply **Facebetter Beauty** to a clip. Adjust it in the effect panel. The picture is processed on this Mac.

Each page has its own reset. Other pages stay as they are.

<Note>
The controls match the [OBS beauty filter](/obs/effects). Those pages also describe selecting a source, signing in, hold-to-compare, and effect presets. None of that applies here. There is no Facebetter account. Purchase and the trial watermark are handled in FxFactory. See [Install](/fcp/install).
</Note>

| Page | What it adjusts |
| --- | --- |
| Skin | Smoothing style, blemish removal, skin tone, rosiness, and clarity. Can be limited to skin. |
| Shape | Face, forehead and brows, eyes, nose, and mouth. Each control moves both ways. 0 leaves it unchanged. |
| Makeup | Lipstick, blush, teeth, contour, eyeshadow, eyeliner, eyebrows, eyelashes, and contacts. Intensity 0 turns that item off. |
| Color | Brightness, contrast, saturation, temperature, and tint, then one LUT. |
| Sticker | One face sticker, screen sticker, or 3D prop. Choose none to remove it. |
| Body | Torso, shoulders and arms, and legs. The person needs to be larger in the frame than a face-only shot before the change is obvious. |
| Background | Keep, blur, or replace the background. Cut out with person detection, or with a green, blue, or red screen. |
