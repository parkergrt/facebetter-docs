The Beauty Effect SDK processes images and live video on Android, iOS, Web, macOS, Windows, and Linux. Processing is GPU-accelerated. Enums are on [Parameter enums](./makeup). Plans are on [Plans & Features](./pricing).

## Core features

### Skin

Smoothing, whitening, rosiness, and blemish removal. Each is `[0.0, 1.0]` (`0` is off).

- Smoothing has three styles: Natural, Texture, and Smooth. The default is Natural.
- Whitening has five looks: Cold white, Pink white, Warm white, Wheat, and Tan. The default is Cold white.
- `setBeautySkinOnly` limits skin beauty to the segmented skin region.
- Blemish removal is independent of smoothing. It uses the skin region, not face landmarks.

### Color grade

Full-frame controls. They do not use face landmarks or the skin mask, and they stay independent of whitening, rosiness, and LUT filters.

Brightness, contrast, saturation, temperature, and tint are `[-1.0, 1.0]` (`0` is neutral). Clarity is `[0.0, 1.0]` (`0` is off) and raises local contrast.

### Teeth

`setTeethWhiten` is `[0.0, 1.0]` (`0` is off). It whitens teeth in an open mouth and leaves a closed mouth unchanged.

### Face reshape

26 controls, each in `[-1.0, 1.0]`. `0` keeps the original proportion. Positive and negative are opposite directions. One frame can reshape and apply makeup on at most **5 faces**. The list is on [Parameter enums](./makeup). Requires **Pro** (Free: watermarked trial). See [Plans & Features](./pricing).

### Body reshape

Nine controls, each in `[0.0, 1.0]` (`0` is off). There is no negative direction.

Seven follow the body: whole-body slim, waist, legs, shoulders, arms, longer legs, and bust. Those need the optional `resource_body.fbd` pack. See [Optional resource packs](./resource-packs).

`LegStretch` and `TorsoLong` use face landmarks only and do not need that pack.

Requires **Pro+** (Free: watermarked trial). See [Plans & Features](./pricing).

### Makeup

Lipstick, blush, contour, eyeshadow, eyeliner, eyebrows, eyelashes, and colored contacts. Intensity is `[0.0, 1.0]`. Shape and color are set separately, with two exceptions:

- Lipstick is one lip mask plus a color. There are six colors and no style swap.
- Contour and contacts swap a texture and are not tinted. There are nine contact colors.

Several makeup items can be on at once. Enums are on [Parameter enums](./makeup). Requires **Pro** (Free: watermarked trial).

### Filters

The library ships **51** LUT filters in four groups: portrait, modern, food, and landscape. Intensity is adjustable. A custom filter is an `.fbd` file passed to `setFilter`. Requires **Pro** (Free: watermarked trial).

### Stickers

- **Screen stickers** stay at a fixed place on the frame.
- **Face stickers** follow face landmarks.
- **3D stickers** are mesh props such as glasses, hats, and masks. They need `resource_3d.fbd`. See [Optional resource packs](./resource-packs).

Resources are `.fbd` files. Apply one 2D sticker with `setSticker` and one 3D sticker with `set3DSticker` (path or bytes). Remove them with `clearSticker` / `clear3DSticker`. The new file applies on the next processed frame. Requires **Pro+** (Free: watermarked trial).

### Virtual background

- **Blur**: `setVirtualBackgroundBlur`. `0` turns it off.
- **Image**: `setVirtualBackground` with a JPEG or PNG path or encoded bytes.
- **Chroma key**: green, blue, or red as the mask. Similarity, edge feather, and spill suppression are separate controls. With no blur or image, the keyed area is transparent.
- **Subject outline**: a stroke around the cutout. It draws when blur, image replace, or chroma key is on.

Requires **Pro+** (Free: watermarked trial).

## Input formats

| Type | Formats |
| --- | --- |
| Still image | JPEG, PNG |
| Video frame | YUV I420, NV12, NV21, RGB, RGBA, BGR, BGRA |
| GPU texture | OpenGL / OpenGL ES texture |

`ImageFrame` converts among the pixel formats.
