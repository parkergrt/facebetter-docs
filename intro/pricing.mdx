This page describes **feature support** and **pricing** for each Facebetter subscription plan. To purchase or renew, use the [Pricing](https://facebetter.net/pricing) page or the dashboard.

## Pricing

One subscription covers all platforms (iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, Linux, Web). Monthly and yearly billing are available; yearly billing is about **20% off** (12 months for the price of 10).

| Plan | Monthly (USD/month) | Yearly (USD/year) | Notes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Free | $0 | $0 | Try most effects with a **permanent watermark**. Unlimited time. No face keypoint callbacks. |
| Basic | $100 | $960 | No watermark. Skin beauty only. Standard support. |
| Pro | $200 | $1,920 | Reshape (26 params), makeup, LUT filters, priority support |
| Pro+ | $400 | $3,840 | Virtual background, stickers, chroma key, face keypoint callbacks |

Exact amounts are shown on the [Pricing](https://facebetter.net/pricing) page or in the dashboard.
