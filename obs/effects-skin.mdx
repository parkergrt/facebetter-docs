Reset on this page returns these controls to their defaults.

How the panel follows the selected source: [Adjust effects](/obs/effects).

## Skin areas only

When this is on, smoothing and tone stay on skin. Clothes, the background, and a product in frame are left alone.

## Smooth

How the smoothing is drawn. Choices: **Texture**, **Natural**, **Smooth**.

- **Smooth** — Strength of smoothing.
- **Blemish** — Fades spots and blemishes.

## Tone

The tone the whitening moves toward. Choices: **Cool**, **Pink**, **Warm**, **Wheat**, **Tan**.

- **Tone** — Strength of that tone.
- **Rosy** — Adds a rosy cast on top of the tone.

## Clarity

- **Clarity** — Brings texture back. Use it when smoothing looks too soft.

0 is off. Higher is stronger.
