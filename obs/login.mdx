Install first: [macOS](/obs/install), [Windows](/obs/install-windows), or [Ubuntu](/obs/install-ubuntu). Sign-in and the panel work the same on all three.

## Sign in

After OBS opens, the Facebetter panel is on the right. If you do not see it, use **Tools → Facebetter**. It can stay docked, like the audio mixer.

1. Click **Sign in**. If you do not have an account, click **Sign up** under it.
2. The system browser opens the Facebetter authorization page. If you are already signed in on the website, it shows that account and does not ask for the password again.
3. Click **Authorize OBS**. To use another account, click **Use a different account**.

Come back to OBS when the browser finishes. The free plan includes every effect. Output carries a `facebetter.net` watermark until you subscribe.

## The panel follows the selected source

Select one source in the OBS Sources list. A camera, image, video, or media source can take the filter. A source with no picture cannot.

The header shows that source, and a short hint:

- **Adjusting will add a Facebetter filter** — this source has no Facebetter filter yet. Move any slider and the filter is added. This is on by default.
- **Hidden — show it to see the effect** — the source is hidden. Show it to see the effect.
- **Unsupported source** — This source has no video, so Facebetter can't be added
- Several sources are selected — pick one.

The switch is in [Preferences](/obs/settings), under **General & startup**: **Auto-add to sources**. Turn it off if you want to add the filter yourself: right-click the source, choose Filters, click +, and choose **Facebetter Beauty**. You can also use the Filters button under the Sources list.

## While you adjust

- **Hold to compare** — hold it to see that source without the effect. The picture you send is also unprocessed while you hold it. Release to go back.
- **Effect presets** — built-in looks, plus **Save as my preset**. **Default** turns the effects off. Saving stores the current controls as your own preset.
- **Reset** on a page returns that page to its defaults. Other pages stay as they are.
- Panel switches, update checks, and how to collect logs are in [Preferences](/obs/settings).

The panel language follows OBS. Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Spanish, and German have their own interface. Other OBS languages show English.

What each page adjusts is in the sidebar under Adjust, starting at [Adjust effects](/obs/effects).

## Remove the watermark

Every control is available on the free plan. A subscription removes the watermark. Open the account button and choose **Get Pro**, or subscribe at [https://facebetter.net/dashboard/obs](https://facebetter.net/dashboard/obs).

Built-in looks: Fresh natural, Cool & clear, Sweet glow, Outdoor sun, Esports look, Default.
