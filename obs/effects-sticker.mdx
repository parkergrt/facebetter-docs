One sticker at a time. **No sticker** removes it. Face stickers follow the face. Screen stickers sit on the frame. 3D props are the glasses. **More** is not available yet.

Reset on this page returns these controls to their defaults.
How the panel follows the selected source: [Adjust effects](/obs/effects).

## 2D face stickers

Glasses, Fox, Kiss, Mustache, Fan club, Falling sakura, Falling pigs

## 2D screen

Butterfly, Petals

## 3D props

3D glasses, Red glasses
