**Outline** draws a white line around the person. It is available when the background is blurred or replaced, not when you keep the original.

Reset on this page returns these controls to their defaults.

How the panel follows the selected source: [Adjust effects](/obs/effects).

## Fill

Choose one:

- **Original**
- **Blur** — Soften what is behind the person. **Blur** sets how strong it is.
- **Image** — Replace the background with a picture. Pick a scene, or **Custom image** and choose a file. Scenes: **Office**, **Living room**, **Study**, **Bedroom**, **Indoor**.

## Cutout

**AI** finds the person. **Green**, **Blue**, and **Red** use a colored screen. Those three show three more sliders:

- **Feather** — Softens the edge of the cutout.
- **Spill** — Pulls the screen color off the person.
- **Similarity** — How much of the nearby color still counts as the screen.
