The first five sliders are centered. 0 leaves that setting unchanged. Drag either way.

Reset on this page returns these controls to their defaults.
How the panel follows the selected source: [Adjust effects](/obs/effects).

## Basic grade

- **Brightness** — Lighter or darker.
- **Contrast** — Stronger or softer difference between light and dark.
- **Saturation** — More or less color.
- **Temperature** — Warmer toward yellow, cooler toward blue.
- **Tint** — Toward green or toward magenta.

## LUT library

**None** clears the look. **Filter** sets how strong the selected look is. The looks are grouped into four categories.
