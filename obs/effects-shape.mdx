These controls reshape the face. They do not change the body. Body controls are on [Body](/obs/effects-body).

Each slider starts at 0, which leaves that feature unchanged. Drag either way.

Reset on this page returns these controls to their defaults.
How the panel follows the selected source: [Adjust effects](/obs/effects).

## Face

**Slim**, **Narrow**, **Small**, **Short**, **V-shape**, **Cheekbone**, **Jaw**, **Chin**

## Brow & forehead

**Forehead**, **Brow height**, **Brow gap**, **Thickness**

## Eyes

**Enlarge**, **Round**, **Distance**, **Height**, **Tilt**, **Inner corner**, **Lower lid**

## Nose

**Slim**, **Length**

## Mouth

**Philtrum**, **Mouth size**, **Height**, **Smile**, **Lips**
